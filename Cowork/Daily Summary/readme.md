# Daily Summary

Three separate Cowork prompts, each scheduled to run **daily** and email the result to me. Run them a few minutes apart (e.g. 6:00, 6:05, 6:10 AM) so the emails arrive in a predictable order.

---

## 1. Daily Email Action Summary

```text
Review all emails I received yesterday (from 12:00 AM to 11:59 PM my local time), including those in subfolders, but excluding newsletters, marketing, and automated notifications unless they require action.

Create a summary organized into these sections:
1. Needs my response – emails where someone asked me a question or is waiting on a reply. Include sender, subject, what they need, and any deadline.
2. Action items for me – tasks I was asked to do or committed to. Include who asked, the task, and the due date if mentioned.
3. FYI / important updates – key information I should know but don't need to act on.

Mark anything urgent or due within 48 hours with [URGENT] and list those first. Include a link to each original email.

Email this summary to me with the subject "Daily Email Action Summary – [yesterday's date]".
```

---

## 2. Daily Teams Action Summary

```text
Review all my Microsoft Teams chats from yesterday (12:00 AM to 11:59 PM my local time), including 1:1 chats, group chats, and channel messages where I was @mentioned.

Create a summary organized into these sections:
1. Needs my response – messages where someone asked me a question or is waiting on my reply. Include who, which chat, what they need, and any deadline.
2. Action items for me – tasks I was assigned or agreed to. Include who asked, the task, and the due date if mentioned.
3. FYI / key decisions – important updates or decisions I should be aware of.

Mark anything urgent or due within 48 hours with [URGENT] and list those first. Include a link to each chat or message.

Email this summary to me with the subject "Daily Teams Action Summary – [yesterday's date]".
```

---

## 3. Daily Meeting Summary

```text
Review all meetings on my calendar from yesterday, including meetings I attended and meetings I was invited to but did not attend.

For each meeting, provide:
- Meeting title, time, and organizer
- Whether I attended or missed it
- A brief summary of what was discussed and any decisions made (use the transcript, recap, notes, or meeting chat if available)
- Action items assigned to me, clearly highlighted with [ACTION FOR ME], including the task, who assigned it, and due date
- Other action items or follow-ups relevant to me

Put a section at the top called "My Action Items" that lists every action item assigned to me across all meetings. List missed meetings with action items for me first. If a meeting has no transcript or notes, say so.

Email this summary to me with the subject "Daily Meeting Summary – [yesterday's date]".
```
