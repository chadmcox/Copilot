# Daily Summary

Three separate Cowork prompts, each scheduled to run **daily** and email the result to me. Run them a few minutes apart (e.g. 6:00, 6:05, 6:10 AM) so the emails arrive in a predictable order.

---

## 1. Daily Email Action Summary

```text
Review all emails I received yesterday (from 12:00 AM to 11:59 PM my local time), including those in subfolders, but excluding newsletters, marketing, and automated notifications unless they require action.

Create a summary organized into these sections:
1. Needs my response – emails where someone asked me a question or is waiting on a reply. Include sender, subject, what they need, and any deadline.
2. Action items for me – tasks I was asked to do or committed to. Include who asked, the task, and the due date if mentioned.
3. FYI / important updates – key information I should know but don't need to act on.

Mark anything urgent or due within 48 hours with [URGENT] and list those first. Include a link to each original email.

Email this summary to me with the subject "Daily Email Action Summary – [yesterday's date]".
```

---

## 2. Daily Teams Action Summary

```text
Review all my Microsoft Teams chats from yesterday (12:00 AM to 11:59 PM my local time), including 1:1 chats, group chats, and channel messages where I was @mentioned.

Create a summary organized into these sections:
1. Needs my response – messages where someone asked me a question or is waiting on my reply. Include who, which chat, what they need, and any deadline.
2. Action items for me – tasks I was assigned or agreed to. Include who asked, the task, and the due date if mentioned.
3. FYI / key decisions – important updates or decisions I should be aware of.

Mark anything urgent or due within 48 hours with [URGENT] and list those first. Include a link to each chat or message.

Email this summary to me with the subject "Daily Teams Action Summary – [yesterday's date]".
```

---

## 3. Daily Meeting Summary

```text
Review all meetings on my calendar from yesterday, including meetings I attended and meetings I was invited to but did not attend.

For each meeting, provide:
- Meeting title, time, and organizer
- Whether I attended or missed it
- A brief summary of what was discussed and any decisions made (use the transcript, recap, notes, or meeting chat if available)
- Action items assigned to me, clearly highlighted with [ACTION FOR ME], including the task, who assigned it, and due date
- Other action items or follow-ups relevant to me

Put a section at the top called "My Action Items" that lists every action item assigned to me across all meetings. List missed meetings with action items for me first. If a meeting has no transcript or notes, say so.

Email this summary to me with the subject "Daily Meeting Summary – [yesterday's date]".
```

## 4. Daily Product Update Digest

```text
Find the top 10-15 most recent and important news items, feature releases, and announcements about the following Microsoft products and technologies:

- Microsoft 365 Copilot
- GitHub Copilot
- Copilot Cowork
- Copilot Studio
- Azure AI Foundry
- Agent 365
- Microsoft Defender XDR
- Project Perception

Focus on updates from roughly the last 7 days, prioritizing the newest. Prefer official sources (Microsoft blogs, Tech Community, Microsoft Learn, the M365 roadmap, GitHub's blog, and official release notes) over third-party coverage. Only include genuine updates — new features, GA/preview releases, roadmap changes, pricing or licensing changes, or notable guidance — not opinion pieces or recycled summaries.

For each item, provide:
- Title – the product name followed by a short headline (e.g. "GitHub Copilot – Agent mode now GA")
- Brief summary – 1-2 sentences on what changed and why it matters
- Link – a direct URL to the source article

Organize the items grouped by product, newest first within each group. If a product has no recent updates, note "No recent updates" for it. Do not repeat an item you have already reported in a previous day's digest if you can tell it's the same announcement.

Email this digest to me with the subject "Daily Product Update Digest – [today's date]".
```

## 5. Daily Certification Study Summary

> To study for a different certification later, change only the exam code and name on the **CERTIFICATION** line below. Everything else stays the same.

```text
CERTIFICATION: AB-100 (Microsoft Agentic AI Architect)

I am studying for the certification above over the next month. Each day, give me 5 topics to learn, drawn from the official skills-measured outline for this exam (look it up on Microsoft Learn to make sure the topics are current and accurate).

Work through the exam's objective areas over the month so that, across daily sessions, I cover the whole outline rather than repeating the same topics. Each day, move on to the next topics in order; pick up where the outline left off the day before.

For each of today's 5 topics, write a short, easy-to-digest lesson in plain, simple language:
- Topic name – the concept or skill
- In plain terms – 2-4 sentences explaining what it is and why it matters, with no jargon (or jargon explained simply)
- Why it's on the exam – 1 sentence on how it fits the certification
- Learn more – a link to the relevant Microsoft Learn page

Keep the whole thing friendly and skimmable — something I can read in a few minutes with coffee. At the end, add one line: "Tomorrow: [the next few topics]" so I know what's coming.

Email this to me with the subject "AB-100 Study – Day [running day number] – [today's date]".
```
