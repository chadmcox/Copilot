# Voyage guest recovery

## Inputs
- [Ship], voyage dates, disruption (e.g., missed port, weather diversion, service outage)

## Steps
1. **Gather** bridge/operations emails, Teams messages and meeting transcripts about the disruption, plus guest-service logs and excursion manifests in SharePoint/OneDrive.
2. **Gather** prior recovery playbooks and compensation guidelines from shared folders.
3. **Analyze**: affected guest groups (booked excursions, suite guests, accessibility needs), cancelled or changed services, and options per the playbook.
4. **Produce** an Excel recovery tracker (group, impact, proposed action, owner, status) and a one-page Word brief for the hotel director.
5. **Draft for approval** guest notice text, a crew Teams post, and a briefing invite for department heads.

## Rules
- Follow the documented compensation guidelines; mark anything outside them "needs approval."
- Property management or reservation systems (PMS) are "(confirm)" until connected.
