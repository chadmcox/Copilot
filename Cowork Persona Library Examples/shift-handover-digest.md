# Shift handover digest

## Inputs
- Site or line name, shift window (e.g., 06:00–18:00 local)
- Optional: shared handover folder in SharePoint/OneDrive

## Steps
1. **Gather** Teams channel and chat messages, emails and meeting transcripts in the shift window that mention the site, line, equipment or open work orders.
2. **Gather** any files updated in the handover folder during the window (downtime logs, quality holds, safety observations).
3. **Analyze**: group items into Safety, Quality, Production/Throughput, Equipment/Maintenance, People/Staffing, Open Actions. De-duplicate repeated mentions. Flag anything unresolved or escalated.
4. **Produce** a one-page Word doc: top 3 things the incoming supervisor must know, a table of open actions (item, owner, due, source), and links to sources.
5. **Draft for approval** a Teams post to the shift channel summarizing the top 3 items. Do not post.

## Rules
- Only cite items found in sources; link each one.
- Mark anything inferred as "to confirm."
- Never post or send without approval.
