### 1. Finance

**Title:** Revenue Accounting Manager  
**Duty:** Manage freight revenue, interline settlements, and demurrage billing.  
**Sources:** Outlook, Teams chats, meeting transcripts, SharePoint/OneDrive, calendar, Dynamics 365, Power BI, ERP (confirm; e.g., SAP), revenue/waybill system (confirm)

1. *Daily:* "List disputed demurrage invoices from customer emails with car counts and dispute reasons" → Excel tracker
2. *Analytical:* "Analyze revenue per carload by commodity versus plan this quarter" → Excel tracker
3. *Leadership:* "Prepare the monthly revenue review deck for the CFO" → PowerPoint deck
4. *Daily:* "Gather interline settlement exceptions from the past week and assign owners" → Excel tracker

## Top 5 custom skills to build first

- **yard-performance-brief** (Trainmaster / Yardmaster; Chief Operating Officer): Gather dwell and car count data → identify late trains → scheduled digest
- **fra-defect-tracker** (Quality / Rules Compliance Manager): Collect defect reports → map due dates → Excel tracker
- **crew-claim-analysis** (Craft Labor Relations Manager): Gather claims → categorize by agreement article → Word doc
- **mechanical-priority-list** (Carman / Locomotive Machinist): Pull detector alerts and inspections → prioritize → Excel tracker
- **shipper-qbr-pack** (Marketing & Sales Manager (Carload/Intermodal)): Gather volume and service data → summarize → PowerPoint deck

## Top 5 connectors to demo

- **Transportation/yard system** (Trainmaster / Yardmaster; Train Dispatcher): A trainmaster gets an overnight yard summary before the 6 a.m. call.
- **Mechanical maintenance system** (Carman / Locomotive Machinist): A shop lead prioritizes bad-order cars quickly.
- **Crew management** (Craft Labor Relations Manager; Conductor): Labor relations sees claim trends by terminal.
- **ERP/finance** (Revenue Accounting Manager): Finance explains revenue per carload variance.
- **Dynamics 365 Sales** (Marketing & Sales Manager (Carload/Intermodal)): Sales preps a shipper QBR from CRM and emails.
