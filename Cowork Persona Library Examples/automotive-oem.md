## Personas and example asks

### 1. Finance

**Title:** Plant Controller / Program Finance Manager  
**Duty:** Manage plant costs, program business cases, and variance reporting.  
**Sources:** Outlook, Teams chats, meeting transcripts, SharePoint/OneDrive, calendar, Power BI, ERP (confirm, e.g., SAP S/4HANA)

1. *Daily:* "Summarize open cost questions from this week's [plant] staff meeting" → Excel action tracker.
2. *Analytical:* "Analyze cost-per-vehicle variances by cost element vs. plan" → Excel variance tracker with commentary.
3. *Leadership:* "Draft the program business case update for [vehicle program]" → PowerPoint deck.
4. *Daily:* "Collect warranty accrual inputs from quality emails" → Excel accrual tracker.
5. *Analytical:* "Compare launch spend vs. budget across programs" → Word variance memo.
6. *Leadership:* "Draft a note to department heads on monthly forecast deadlines" → draft email for approval.

## Top 5 custom skills to build first

- **shortage-daily-digest** (Supply Chain/Procurement; Executive): Gather shortage emails → rank by line impact → produce digest drafted for approval.
- **tier-meeting-brief** (Frontline Supervisor): Collect handovers and KPIs → find top issues → produce Word brief.
- **warranty-trend-review** (Quality; Field Service/Repair): Gather claims and cases → analyze trends → produce Word report.
- **launch-readiness-pack** (Launch Management (industry-specific)): Collect issues and readiness → score status → produce PowerPoint deck.
- **dealer-visit-brief** (Sales/Marketing): Gather dealer emails and performance → summarize → produce Word brief.

## Top 5 connectors to demo

- **ERP (confirm)** (Supply Chain/Procurement; Finance): Planner combines shortage emails with stock to protect the build schedule.
- **MES (confirm)** (Frontline Supervisor): Group leader builds tier brief from downtime and quality data.
- **PLM (confirm)** (Engineering/R&D/Technology): Engineer tracks change status before the gateway review.
- **Warranty system (confirm)** (Quality): Quality engineer links claims to build dates for root cause.
- **DMS (confirm)** (Sales/Marketing): Sales manager preps dealer visits with retail performance.
