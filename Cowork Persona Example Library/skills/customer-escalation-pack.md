# Customer escalation pack

## Inputs
- Customer or account name, escalation topic, lookback window (default 90 days)

## Steps
1. **Gather** emails, Teams chats and meeting transcripts with or about the customer in the window.
2. **Gather** CRM records (cases, opportunities, orders) from Dynamics 365 if connected, plus service reports in SharePoint/OneDrive.
3. **Analyze**: build a timeline of the issue, commitments made and missed, open cases and owners, and the customer's stated asks in their words.
4. **Produce** a PowerPoint deck (5 slides: situation, timeline, open items, options, recommended response) and an Excel action log.
5. **Draft for approval** a holding reply to the customer and a meeting invite for an internal response huddle.

## Rules
- Quote the customer's asks verbatim with the source.
- Don't commit dates or credits in drafts; leave them as [to confirm].
