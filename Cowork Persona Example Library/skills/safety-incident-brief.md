# Safety incident brief

## Inputs
- Incident date, site and short description; optional incident report file

## Steps
1. **Gather** the incident report, related emails, Teams messages and meeting transcripts from the incident date onward.
2. **Gather** past similar incidents or near-misses from shared EHS folders (last 12 months).
3. **Analyze**: build a timeline, immediate actions taken, open corrective actions with owners, and any repeat patterns. Separate facts from statements still under investigation.
4. **Produce** a Word brief: summary, timeline, actions table, pattern note, open questions.
5. **Draft for approval** a leadership update email and a toolbox-talk Teams post for frontline crews.

## Rules
- Do not speculate on root cause; label anything unconfirmed.
- Exclude personal health details; refer to people by role.
- Never send or post without approval.
