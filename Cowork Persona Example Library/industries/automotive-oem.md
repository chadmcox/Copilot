## How to read this page

Each persona has three asks:

- **Quick win:** a small, everyday job that pays off right away.
- **Hand-off:** a multi-step job across several sources that would otherwise take an hour or more.
- **Recurring:** something scheduled or ongoing that keeps working without the person.

Every persona can use the standard Microsoft 365 sources (Outlook, Teams, meeting transcripts, SharePoint/OneDrive, calendar). **Systems** lists only the industry-specific systems that persona uses beyond those, marked *(confirm)* because they vary by company. Anything that sends, posts or schedules is a draft for the person's approval.

**Why the two industry personas:** Launch Management and Connected Vehicle & Software are added because model launches and software-defined vehicles are central OEM priorities.

## Personas and example asks

### 1. Finance

**Title:** Plant Controller / Program Finance Manager  
**Duty:** Manage plant costs, program business cases, and variance reporting.  
**Systems:** Power BI, ERP (confirm; e.g., SAP S/4HANA)

- **Quick win:** "What cost questions came up in this week's [plant] staff meeting?" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Pull cost-per-vehicle actuals by cost element from the ERP, compare to plan, read the warranty accrual inputs from quality, and build the variance slides for the plant review" → PowerPoint deck
- **Recurring:** "Each month before the forecast deadline, draft a reminder to department heads listing who hasn't submitted" → email draft

### 2. HR

**Title:** HR Business Partner, Manufacturing  
**Duty:** Support plant workforce planning, labor relations, and training.  
**Systems:** HRIS (confirm; e.g., Workday or SAP SuccessFactors)

- **Quick win:** "List open team member and skilled trades reqs for [plant]" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Gather 8 weeks of absenteeism by shift, training certification coverage and the staffing plan, find where we're thin, and prepare talking points for the labor-management meeting" → Word doc
- **Recurring:** "Every Friday, summarize onboarding questions new hires asked in Teams and draft answers for the FAQ" → Word doc

### 3. Frontline Worker

**Title:** Production Team Member (Assembly Line)  
**Duty:** Perform standardized work at assigned stations on the assembly line.  
**Systems:** MES (confirm)

- **Quick win:** "What standardized work changes hit my station this week?" → Word doc (one page)
- **Hand-off:** "Find the latest job instruction sheets for the stations I rotate to and check them against my training records" → Excel workbook
- **Recurring:** "At the start of each shift, list quality alerts affecting my zone from the last five shifts" → scheduled digest

### 4. Frontline Supervisor

**Title:** Group Leader / Area Manager (Body, Paint, or Final Assembly)  
**Duty:** Lead shift output, quality, and safety in assigned area.  
**Systems:** MES (confirm; e.g., Siemens Opcenter)

- **Quick win:** "Who's absent today and does my rotation plan still cover every station?" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Pull four weeks of downtime and first-time-through by station, find the top three losses, and prepare my tier 2 meeting update" → PowerPoint deck
- **Recurring:** "At the start of each shift, build my brief from handovers, downtime and quality alerts" → Word doc (one page)

Related example skill: [shift-handover-digest](../skills/shift-handover-digest.md)

### 5. Quality

**Title:** Vehicle Quality Engineer / Plant Quality Manager  
**Duty:** Drive defect containment, root cause, and warranty reduction.  
**Systems:** QMS (confirm), warranty system (confirm)

- **Quick win:** "List open concerns from today's quality review with containment status" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Match field claims for [issue] to build dates, plant audit defects and supplier quality emails, and prepare the plant quality review" → PowerPoint deck
- **Recurring:** "Every Monday, trend audit defects per unit and warranty claims by top issue" → scheduled digest

### 6. Supply Chain/Procurement

**Title:** Material Planner / Purchasing Buyer  
**Duty:** Secure parts supply, manage suppliers, and prevent line stoppages.  
**Systems:** ERP (confirm; e.g., SAP)

- **Quick win:** "What shortages came in by email today and what's being expedited?" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Compare the next two weeks of build schedule with part availability, supplier delivery performance and premium freight spend, and draft the supplier QBR deck for [supplier]" → PowerPoint deck
- **Recurring:** "Every morning, turn shortage emails into an expedite list and draft recovery-plan requests to the suppliers" → Excel workbook + email drafts

Related example skill: [supplier-risk-tracker](../skills/supplier-risk-tracker.md)

- **Recurring:** "Before any planned conveyor downtime, draft the notice to production" → email draft

## Top 5 custom skills to build first

- **shortage-daily-digest** (Supply Chain/Procurement; Executive): Gather shortage emails → rank by line impact → produce scheduled digest. Start from [supplier-risk-tracker](../skills/supplier-risk-tracker.md).
- **tier-meeting-brief** (Frontline Supervisor): Collect handovers and KPIs → find top issues → produce Word brief. Start from [shift-handover-digest](../skills/shift-handover-digest.md).
- **warranty-trend-review** (Quality; Field Service/Repair): Gather claims and cases → analyze trends → produce Word report.
- **launch-readiness-pack** (Launch Management (industry-specific)): Collect issues and readiness → score status → produce PowerPoint deck.
- **dealer-visit-brief** (Sales/Marketing): Gather dealer emails and performance → summarize → produce Word brief.

## Top 5 connectors to demo

- **ERP (confirm)** (Supply Chain/Procurement; Finance): Planner combines shortage emails with stock to protect the build schedule.
- **MES (confirm)** (Frontline Supervisor): Group leader builds tier brief from downtime and quality data.
- **PLM (confirm)** (Engineering/R&D/Technology): Engineer tracks change status before the gateway review.
- **Warranty system (confirm)** (Quality): Quality engineer links claims to build dates for root cause.
- **DMS (confirm)** (Sales/Marketing): Sales manager preps dealer visits with retail performance.
