## How to read this page

Each persona has three asks:

- **Quick win:** a small, everyday job that pays off right away.
- **Hand-off:** a multi-step job across several sources that would otherwise take an hour or more.
- **Recurring:** something scheduled or ongoing that keeps working without the person.

Every persona can use the standard Microsoft 365 sources (Outlook, Teams, meeting transcripts, SharePoint/OneDrive, calendar). **Systems** lists only the industry-specific systems that persona uses beyond those, marked *(confirm)* because they vary by company. Anything that sends, posts or schedules is a draft for the person's approval.

**Why the two industry personas:** Train dispatchers and intermodal terminal managers run core rail-specific workflows (line capacity and container throughput) not covered by the standard personas.

## Personas and example asks

### 1. Finance

**Title:** Revenue Accounting Manager  
**Duty:** Manage freight revenue, interline settlements, and demurrage billing.  
**Systems:** Power BI, ERP (confirm; e.g., SAP), revenue/waybill system (confirm)

- **Quick win:** "Which demurrage invoices are disputed, with car counts and reasons?" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Analyze revenue per carload by commodity vs. plan, add interline settlement variances, and prepare the monthly revenue review deck for the CFO" → PowerPoint deck
- **Recurring:** "Every Monday, list interline settlement items waiting on other railroads" → scheduled digest

### 2. HR

**Title:** Craft Labor Relations Manager  
**Duty:** Administer collective bargaining agreements, crew claims, and union relations.  
**Systems:** HRIS (confirm), crew management/timekeeping system (confirm)

- **Quick win:** "What time claims were filed this week, by craft and agreement article?" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Analyze claim trends by terminal, find recurring agreement interpretations, and draft the briefing for leadership ahead of union negotiations" → Word doc
- **Recurring:** "Every Friday, summarize new claims and decisions by agreement article" → scheduled digest

### 3. Frontline Worker

**Title:** Conductor  
**Duty:** Oversee train operations, paperwork, switching, and crew safety on assignments.

## Top 5 custom skills to build first

- **yard-performance-brief** (Trainmaster / Yardmaster; Chief Operating Officer): Gather dwell and car count data → identify late trains → scheduled digest. Start from [shift-handover-digest](../skills/shift-handover-digest.md).

- **mechanical-priority-list** (Carman / Locomotive Machinist): Pull detector alerts and inspections → prioritize → Excel workbook

## Top 5 connectors to demo

- **Transportation/yard system** (Trainmaster / Yardmaster; Train Dispatcher): A trainmaster gets an overnight yard summary before the 6 a.m. call.
- **Mechanical maintenance system** (Carman / Locomotive Machinist): A shop lead prioritizes bad-order cars quickly.
- **Crew management** (Craft Labor Relations Manager; Conductor): Labor relations sees claim trends by terminal.
- **ERP/finance** (Revenue Accounting Manager): Finance explains revenue per carload variance.
- **Dynamics 365 Sales** (Marketing & Sales Manager (Carload/Intermodal)): Sales preps a shipper QBR from CRM and emails.

## Confirm with each customer

- Which transportation, crew, and dispatch systems are used
- Union agreements limiting data use
- PTC and operational data access restrictions
- Class I vs. short line scope
- Field device and Teams access for crews
- Records retention for FRA reporting
