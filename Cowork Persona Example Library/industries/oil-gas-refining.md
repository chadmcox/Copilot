## How to read this page

Each persona has three asks:

- **Quick win:** a small, everyday job that pays off right away.
- **Hand-off:** a multi-step job across several sources that would otherwise take an hour or more.
- **Recurring:** something scheduled or ongoing that keeps working without the person.

Every persona can use the standard Microsoft 365 sources (Outlook, Teams, meeting transcripts, SharePoint/OneDrive, calendar). **Systems** lists only the industry-specific systems that persona uses beyond those, marked *(confirm)* because they vary by company. Anything that sends, posts or schedules is a draft for the person's approval.

**Why the two industry personas:** Added Production/Process Operations Engineer because uptime and hydrocarbon accounting drive refinery and upstream economics, and Turnaround (TAR) Manager because planned shutdowns are the largest controllable cost and safety exposure.

## Personas and example asks

### 1. Finance

**Title:** Refinery Controller / Upstream Cost Accountant  
**Duty:** Manage AFE tracking, LOE reporting, and month-end close for assets.  
**Systems:** Power BI, ERP (confirm; e.g., SAP S/4HANA), JIB/upstream accounting system (confirm)

- **Quick win:** "Which AFEs are over budget today?" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Compare LOE per BOE by field for three months, find the drivers, and draft the month-end margin commentary for the CFO" → Word doc
- **Recurring:** "Every Monday, list open JIB disputes and partner emails awaiting reply" → scheduled digest

### 2. HR

**Title:** HR Business Partner – Operations  
**Duty:** Support shift workforce planning, competency, and labor relations at sites.  
**Systems:** HCM (confirm; e.g., SAP SuccessFactors/Workday), LMS/competency system (confirm)

- **Quick win:** "Which operators have HUET, H2S or BOSIET certifications expiring this month?" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Analyze overtime and fatigue hours by crew at [plant], and draft the workforce update on the rotation schedule change" → Word doc
- **Recurring:** "Before each rotation, summarize new-hire onboarding status" → scheduled digest

### 3. Frontline Worker

**Title:** Field / Console Operator  
**Duty:** Run process units, perform rounds, and respond to alarms safely.  
**Systems:** Operator rounds/logbook system (confirm), DCS/historian (confirm; e.g., AVEVA PI)

- **Quick win:** "What permits are active in my area today?" → Word doc (one page)
- **Hand-off:** "Pull the last shift's log, open alarms and equipment with repeated alarms this week into one page" → Word doc (one page)
- **Recurring:** "At the end of each shift, draft my log summary and notes for my supervisor" → Word doc (one page)

Related example skill: [shift-handover-digest](../skills/shift-handover-digest.md)

## Top 5 custom skills to build first

- **shift-handover-brief** (Frontline Supervisor; Frontline Worker): Gather logs, chats, open work orders → flag safety and production issues → produce Word handover. Start from [shift-handover-digest](../skills/shift-handover-digest.md).
- **afe-variance-tracker** (Finance): Pull AFE extract and emails → compute variance → produce Excel workbook and commentary.
- **moc-pssr-overdue-review** (Safety/EHS; Engineering): Gather MOC lists → identify overdue items → draft follow-up email for approval. Start from [safety-incident-brief](../skills/safety-incident-brief.md).
- **tar-daily-report** (Turnaround Manager): Collect progress and safety notes → compare to schedule → produce Word report.
- **deferment-analysis** (Production Engineer; Operations): Gather production data → categorize causes → produce Excel analysis and deck.

## Top 5 connectors to demo

- **ERP (confirm)** (Finance; Procurement): Controller reconciles AFE spend without leaving Teams.
- **CMMS (confirm)** (Maintenance; Supervisor; TAR): Technician gets daily work list with permits ready.
- **Historian/SCADA (confirm)** (Process Engineer; Production Engineer): Engineer turns unit trends into a decision memo.
- **EHS system (confirm)** (HSE Manager): HSE Manager drafts daily incident brief in minutes.
- **LIMS (confirm)** (Quality): Lab manager spots off-spec tanks before shipment.

## Confirm with each customer

- Which ERP, CMMS, historian, EHS, and LIMS are in use
- Whether OT data (DCS/SCADA) can be exposed to Microsoft 365
- Upstream vs. downstream scope
- Union and shift-worker device access
- Data residency and export-control constraints
- Approval workflows for external communications
