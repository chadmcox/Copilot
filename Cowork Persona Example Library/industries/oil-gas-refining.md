## Personas and example asks

### 1. Finance

**Title:** Refinery Controller / Upstream Cost Accountant  
**Duty:** Manage AFE tracking, LOE reporting, and month-end close for assets.  
**Sources:** Outlook, Teams chats, meeting transcripts, SharePoint/OneDrive, calendar, Dynamics 365, Power BI; ERP (confirm, e.g., SAP S/4HANA); JIB/upstream accounting system (confirm)

1. *Daily:* "Summarize today's AFE overruns from emails and the ERP extract" → Excel tracker of AFEs over budget with owners.
2. *Analytical:* "Compare LOE per BOE by field for the last 3 months and flag drivers" → Excel analysis with variance commentary.
3. *Leadership:* "Draft the month-end margin commentary for the CFO" → Word doc with crack-spread and opex narrative.
4. *Daily:* "List open JIB disputes and partner emails awaiting reply" → draft email to partners for approval.
5. *Analytical:* "Reconcile accruals vs. vendor invoices for [site] turnaround spend" → Excel tracker with unmatched items.
6. *Leadership:* "Build the quarterly cost-review deck for the asset leadership team" → PowerPoint deck with variances and actions.

## Top 5 custom skills to build first

- **shift-handover-brief** (Frontline Supervisor; Frontline Worker): Gather logs, chats, open work orders → flag safety and production issues → produce Word handover.
- **afe-variance-tracker** (Finance): Pull AFE extract and emails → compute variance → produce Excel tracker and commentary.
- **moc-pssr-overdue-review** (Safety/EHS; Engineering): Gather MOC lists → identify overdue items → draft follow-up email for approval.
- **tar-daily-report** (Turnaround Manager): Collect progress and safety notes → compare to schedule → produce Word report.
- **deferment-analysis** (Production Engineer; Operations): Gather production data → categorize causes → produce Excel analysis and deck.

## Top 5 connectors to demo

- **ERP (confirm)** (Finance; Procurement): Controller reconciles AFE spend without leaving Teams.
- **CMMS (confirm)** (Maintenance; Supervisor; TAR): Technician gets daily work list with permits ready.
- **Historian/SCADA (confirm)** (Process Engineer; Production Engineer): Engineer turns unit trends into a decision memo.
- **EHS system (confirm)** (HSE Manager): HSE Manager drafts daily incident brief in minutes.
- **LIMS (confirm)** (Quality): Lab manager spots off-spec tanks before shipment.

## Confirm with each customer

- Which ERP, CMMS, historian, EHS, and LIMS are in use
- Whether OT data (DCS/SCADA) can be exposed to Microsoft 365
- Upstream vs. downstream scope
- Union and shift-worker device access
- Data residency and export-control constraints
- Approval workflows for external communications
