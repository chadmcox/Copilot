## Top 5 custom skills to build first

- **backorder-customer-notify** (Customer Service; Supply Chain/Procurement): Gather backorders and PO ETAs → match customers → draft notifications for approval.
- **vendor-line-review** (Supply Chain/Procurement; Quality): Pull fill rate, returns, rebates → score vendor → build review deck.
- **shift-productivity-review** (Frontline Supervisor; Operations/Facilities): Collect warehouse metrics → analyze by associate → produce tracker and huddle post.
- **margin-leak-scan** (Pricing & Margin Analyst; Finance): Find quotes under floor → compare to cost changes → produce tracker and sales note.
- **customer-business-review** (Sales/Marketing; Vendor-Managed Inventory Specialist): Gather sales and consumption data → analyze savings → build customer deck.

## Top 5 connectors to demo

- **ERP (confirm)** (Finance; Supply Chain/Procurement; Customer Service): Reps answer order-status questions without switching across screens.
- **WMS (confirm)** (Frontline Supervisor; Operations/Facilities): Supervisors get a backlog and mispick summary at shift start.
- **Pricing engine (confirm)** (Pricing & Margin Analyst): Analysts catch below-floor quotes every day.
- **Vending/VMI system (confirm)** (Vendor-Managed Inventory Specialist): Specialists plan replenishment routes from one ask.
- **EHS incident system (confirm)** (Safety/EHS): EHS sees weekly incident trends across DCs without manual consolidation.
