## How to read this page

Each persona has three asks:

- **Quick win:** a small, everyday job that pays off right away.
- **Hand-off:** a multi-step job across several sources that would otherwise take an hour or more.
- **Recurring:** something scheduled or ongoing that keeps working without the person.

Every persona can use the standard Microsoft 365 sources (Outlook, Teams, meeting transcripts, SharePoint/OneDrive, calendar). **Systems** lists only the industry-specific systems that persona uses beyond those, marked *(confirm)* because they vary by company. Anything that sends, posts or schedules is a draft for the person's approval.

**Why the two industry personas:** Pricing Analyst protects thin distribution margins through quote and contract pricing; VMI Specialist runs onsite customer inventory programs, a key differentiator in industrial distribution.

## Personas and example asks

### 1. Finance

**Title:** Controller / Credit & Collections Manager  
**Duty:** Manage AR, customer credit, vendor rebates, and branch margin.  
**Systems:** Power BI, ERP (confirm; e.g., SAP, Epicor, Infor), rebate management (confirm)

- **Quick win:** "Which customers are over credit limit with orders on hold, and why?" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Analyze supplier rebate accruals vs. expected by vendor program and DSO trends, and draft the CFO memo on collection risk with three actions" → Word doc
- **Recurring:** "Every Monday, list open pricing and deduction disputes from email" → scheduled digest

### 2. HR

**Duty:** Hire and retain warehouse, counter, and outside sales staff.  
**Systems:** HRIS (confirm), workforce scheduling (confirm)

## Top 5 custom skills to build first

- **backorder-customer-notify** (Customer Service; Supply Chain/Procurement): Gather backorders and PO ETAs → match customers → draft notifications for approval. Start from [customer-escalation-pack](../skills/customer-escalation-pack.md).
- **vendor-line-review** (Supply Chain/Procurement; Quality): Pull fill rate, returns, rebates → score vendor → build review deck. Start from [supplier-risk-tracker](../skills/supplier-risk-tracker.md).
- **shift-productivity-review** (Frontline Supervisor; Operations/Facilities): Collect warehouse metrics → analyze by associate → produce tracker and huddle post. Start from [shift-handover-digest](../skills/shift-handover-digest.md).
- **margin-leak-scan** (Pricing & Margin Analyst; Finance): Find quotes under floor → compare to cost changes → produce tracker and sales note.
- **customer-business-review** (Sales/Marketing; Vendor-Managed Inventory Specialist): Gather sales and consumption data → analyze savings → build customer deck.

## Top 5 connectors to demo

- **ERP (confirm)** (Finance; Supply Chain/Procurement; Customer Service): Reps answer order-status questions without switching across screens.
- **WMS (confirm)** (Frontline Supervisor; Operations/Facilities): Supervisors get a backlog and mispick summary at shift start.
- **Pricing engine (confirm)** (Pricing & Margin Analyst): Analysts catch below-floor quotes every day.
- **Vending/VMI system (confirm)** (Vendor-Managed Inventory Specialist): Specialists plan replenishment routes from one ask.
- **EHS incident system (confirm)** (Safety/EHS): EHS sees weekly incident trends across DCs without manual consolidation.
