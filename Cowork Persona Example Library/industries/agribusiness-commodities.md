## How to read this page

Each persona has a **quick win**, a **hand-off** and a **recurring** ask. Systems marked *(confirm)* vary by company. Anything that sends, posts or schedules is a draft. Details: [How to use](../README.md#how-to-use).

**Why the two industry personas:** Commodity Trader: price risk is core to the business. Grower Relations Manager: supply depends on grower loyalty.

## Personas and example asks

### 1. Finance

**Title:** Commodity Accounting Manager  
**Duty:** Manage inventory valuation, mark-to-market, and hedge accounting.  
**Systems:** Power BI, CTRM (confirm; e.g., ION/Openlink, Eka)

- **Quick win:** "Which commodities had mark-to-market moves over threshold today?" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Pull basis gains and losses by elevator from the CTRM, compare storage and drying costs by site, and build the month-end close deck on hedge effectiveness and inventory" → PowerPoint deck
- **Recurring:** "Every Monday, list unsettled grower contracts and payment holds with age" → scheduled digest

### 2. HR

**Title:** HR Generalist – Operations  
**Duty:** Support seasonal hiring, safety training, and site workforce.  
**Systems:** HRIS (confirm; e.g., Workday)

- **Quick win:** "Which harvest seasonal hires are missing grain-bin entry training?" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Gather turnover, overtime and staffing data by site from last harvest, read the site managers' notes, and write the harvest staffing plan for region leadership" → Word doc
- **Recurring:** "Each week during harvest, check onboarding tasks still open and draft reminders to site managers" → email drafts

### 3. Frontline Worker

**Title:** Grain Elevator Operator  
**Duty:** Receive, grade, store, and load grain at elevator.  
**Systems:** Grain accounting/elevator system (confirm; e.g., AGRIS)

- **Quick win:** "What's the current discount schedule for moisture and damage on corn?" → Word doc (one page)
- **Hand-off:** "Compare my scale tickets with grading results for this week and list any that don't match" → Excel workbook
- **Recurring:** "At the end of each shift, draft my handover with bin levels, receipts and equipment issues" → Word doc (one page)

Related example skill: [shift-handover-digest](../skills/shift-handover-digest.md)

### 4. Frontline Supervisor

**Title:** Elevator/Location Manager  
**Duty:** Run location operations, grower service, storage, and shipping.  
**Systems:** Grain accounting/elevator system (confirm; e.g., AGRIS)

- **Quick win:** "Brief me on today's inbound appointments, rail cars and staffing" → Word doc (one page)
- **Hand-off:** "Pull this month's grade and shrink data by bin, the dryer logs and the grading disputes, find where we're losing value, and write the location review" → Word doc + Excel workbook
- **Recurring:** "Every Monday during harvest, draft a safety huddle post on engulfment hazards with last week's near misses" → Teams post draft

### 5. Quality

**Title:** Grain Quality & Food Safety Lead  
**Duty:** Ensure grade standards, mycotoxin testing, and FSMA compliance.  
**Systems:** LIMS (confirm)

- **Quick win:** "Which loads this week had aflatoxin or vomitoxin results above limits?" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Gather lab results, customer quality complaints in Dynamics 365 and the blending logs, find the patterns by origin, and build the FSMA audit-readiness deck" → PowerPoint deck
- **Recurring:** "Every Friday, trend grading disputes by site and grader" → scheduled digest

### 6. Supply Chain/Procurement

**Title:** Logistics & Merchandising Coordinator  
**Duty:** Coordinate rail, truck, barge logistics and origination.  
**Systems:** CTRM (confirm; e.g., ION/Openlink, Eka), TMS (confirm)

- **Quick win:** "Which sales contracts ship this week without assigned freight?" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Pull freight cost per bushel by mode and lane, carrier delay and demurrage emails, and next month's barge vs. rail spreads, and build the logistics review" → PowerPoint deck
- **Recurring:** "Every morning, summarize carrier emails about delays and demurrage" → scheduled digest

Related example skill: [supplier-risk-tracker](../skills/supplier-risk-tracker.md)

### 7. Safety/EHS

**Title:** Regional Safety Manager  
**Duty:** Drive OSHA grain-handling compliance, dust control, and training.  
**Systems:** EHS system (confirm)

- **Quick win:** "List overdue bin-entry and lockout audits by site" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Pull near misses, incidents and training completion by site and season, find the sites with the biggest gaps, and build the pre-harvest safety readiness deck" → PowerPoint deck
- **Recurring:** "Every Friday, summarize the week's dust housekeeping inspections" → scheduled digest

Related example skill: [safety-incident-brief](../skills/safety-incident-brief.md)

### 8. Field Service/Repair

**Title:** Facility Maintenance Technician  
**Duty:** Maintain dryers, conveyors, legs, and scales.  
**Systems:** CMMS (confirm)

- **Quick win:** "Show my open work orders ranked by harvest impact" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Find past fixes for bucket elevator belt misalignment in work orders, emails and vendor manuals, and pull them into one troubleshooting guide" → Word doc
- **Recurring:** "Each month before harvest, list preventive maintenance due and parts I need to order" → scheduled digest

### 9. Sales/Marketing

**Title:** Grain Merchandiser  
**Duty:** Buy grain from growers and sell to end users.  
**Systems:** Dynamics 365, CTRM (confirm; e.g., ION/Openlink, Eka)

- **Quick win:** "Summarize today's bids, basis and open grower offers" → Word doc (one page)
- **Hand-off:** "Compare grower contracting pace to last year and our basis to nearby competitors, and build the deck for the next grower meeting" → PowerPoint deck
- **Recurring:** "Every Monday, list grower contracts expiring in the next 30 days" → scheduled digest

### 10. Customer Service

**Title:** Grower Services Representative  
**Duty:** Handle grower settlements, contracts, and account questions.  
**Systems:** Dynamics 365, Grain accounting/elevator system (confirm; e.g., AGRIS)

- **Quick win:** "Which settlements are pending and what documents are missing?" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Before my call with [grower], gather their contracts, settlements, past emails and call notes into one account brief" → Word doc (one page)
- **Recurring:** "Every Friday, summarize common grower inquiries for the merchandisers" → scheduled digest

Related example skill: [customer-escalation-pack](../skills/customer-escalation-pack.md)

### 11. Operations/Facilities

**Title:** Regional Operations Manager  
**Duty:** Oversee locations, storage capacity, and throughput.  
**Systems:** Grain accounting/elevator system (confirm; e.g., AGRIS), ERP (confirm; e.g., SAP, Microsoft Dynamics 365 F&O)

- **Quick win:** "What were today's receipts, shipments and incidents by location?" → Word doc (one page)
- **Hand-off:** "Pull storage utilization, drying energy cost and throughput by site, compare to last year, and build the quarterly operations review" → PowerPoint deck
- **Recurring:** "Every morning during harvest, send a digest of receipts, shipments, storage and incidents by location" → scheduled digest

### 12. Engineering/R&D/Technology

**Title:** Agronomist / Ag Technology Specialist  
**Duty:** Advise growers on crops and run precision ag programs.  
**Systems:** Farm management platform (confirm; e.g., Climate FieldView, John Deere Operations Center)

- **Quick win:** "Which of my growers are due for soil sampling?" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Gather yield trial results by hybrid and soil type, my scouting notes and last season's input recommendations, and build the winter grower meeting deck" → PowerPoint deck
- **Recurring:** "Every Friday during the season, summarize my crop scouting notes by grower" → scheduled digest

### 13. Executive

**Title:** President, Grain Division  
**Duty:** Lead division strategy, risk, and profitability.  
**Systems:** Power BI, CTRM (confirm; e.g., ION/Openlink, Eka)

- **Quick win:** "What decisions are still open from the last risk committee?" → Word doc (one page)
- **Hand-off:** "Pull margin per bushel by region and commodity, capital project returns and safety results, and build the board deck on division performance and risk" → PowerPoint deck
- **Recurring:** "Every Friday, summarize positions, margins, safety and capacity for the week" → scheduled digest

### 14. Commodity Trader (industry-specific)

**Title:** Commodity Trader / Risk Analyst  
**Duty:** Manage positions, hedges, and risk limits.  
**Systems:** CTRM (confirm; e.g., ION/Openlink, Eka)

- **Quick win:** "Summarize my open positions and limit usage" → Word doc (one page)
- **Hand-off:** "Compare hedge ratios to physical inventory by location, find gaps over policy, and draft the risk committee update" → Word doc + Excel workbook
- **Recurring:** "Every morning before the open, summarize market commentary from email and my position changes" → scheduled digest

### 15. Grower Relations (industry-specific)

**Title:** Grower Relations / Origination Manager  
**Duty:** Build grower relationships and secure supply.  
**Systems:** Grain accounting/elevator system (confirm; e.g., AGRIS)

- **Quick win:** "Which growers haven't I contacted in 30 days?" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Pull acres contracted vs. target by county, share-of-wallet data and my visit notes, and build a grower visit plan for the next month" → Excel workbook + meeting invite draft
- **Recurring:** "Before each grower visit, summarize our last meeting notes and open items with that grower" → Word doc (one page)

## Top 5 custom skills to build first

- **daily-position-brief** (Trader, Executive): Gather CTRM exports and email → summarize positions → produce digest. Start from [shift-handover-digest](../skills/shift-handover-digest.md).
- **harvest-readiness-pack** (Regional Ops, Safety): Gather maintenance, training, staffing → find gaps → produce deck.
- **grower-settlement-tracker** (Grower Services): Pull settlements → flag missing docs → produce Excel workbook. Start from [customer-escalation-pack](../skills/customer-escalation-pack.md).
- **freight-exception-review** (Logistics Coordinator): Gather carrier emails → match to contracts → draft email.
- **grower-touch-planner** (Grower Relations, Merchandiser): Review Dynamics 365 contacts → find gaps → draft invites.

## Top 5 connectors to demo

- **CTRM (confirm)** (Trader, Accounting, Merchandiser): Trader gets a positions summary with market commentary.
- **Grain accounting system (confirm)** (Elevator Operator, Location Manager): Location manager sees bins near capacity before harvest rush.
- **TMS (confirm)** (Logistics Coordinator): Coordinator matches late rail cars to contracts.
- **LIMS (confirm)** (Quality Lead): Quality lead finds mycotoxin hotspots by origin.
- **Farm management platform (confirm)** (Agronomist): Agronomist builds grower meeting deck from trial data.

## Confirm with each customer

- Which CTRM, grain accounting, LIMS are used
- Hedge accounting and position-limit policies
- Frontline device and connectivity at rural sites
- Grower data privacy agreements
- Seasonal workforce licensing
- Regulatory scope (CFTC, USDA FGIS, FSMA)
