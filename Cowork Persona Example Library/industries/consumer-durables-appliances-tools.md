## How to read this page

Each persona has three asks:

- **Quick win:** a small, everyday job that pays off right away.
- **Hand-off:** a multi-step job across several sources that would otherwise take an hour or more.
- **Recurring:** something scheduled or ongoing that keeps working without the person.

Every persona can use the standard Microsoft 365 sources (Outlook, Teams, meeting transcripts, SharePoint/OneDrive, calendar). **Systems** lists only the industry-specific systems that persona uses beyond those, marked *(confirm)* because they vary by company. Anything that sends, posts or schedules is a draft for the person's approval.

**Why the two industry personas:** Product Safety & Regulatory Manager (recall and certification risk is industry-defining) and Demand Planner (retail seasonality drives S&OP).

## Personas and example asks

### 1. Finance

**Title:** Product Line Finance Manager  
**Duty:** Manages P&L, landed cost, and promotional spend by product category.  
**Systems:** Power BI, ERP (confirm; e.g., SAP), trade promotion system (confirm)

- **Quick win:** "Which retailer deductions and chargebacks came in this week?" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Analyze landed cost and tariff impact by SKU family, compare holiday promo ROI by retailer, and draft the monthly category P&L commentary" → Word doc
- **Recurring:** "Every Friday, list co-op and MDF accrual questions from Teams" → scheduled digest

### 2. HR

**Title:** HR Business Partner, Manufacturing & Distribution  
**Duty:** Supports staffing, retention, and engagement across plants and DCs.  
**Systems:** HRIS (confirm; e.g., Workday), timekeeping (confirm)

## Top 5 custom skills to build first

- **retailer-line-review-prep** (Retail Account Manager; Demand Planner): Gather POS, emails, CRM → analyze sell-through → produce Word brief and deck.
- **returns-failure-trend** (Product Quality Engineer; Product Design Engineer): Collect returns and cases → group by failure code → produce PowerPoint deck. Start from [customer-escalation-pack](../skills/customer-escalation-pack.md).
- **safety-incident-triage** (Product Safety & Regulatory Manager; Consumer Care Agent): Gather incident cases → assess risk signals → produce Excel workbook. Start from [safety-incident-brief](../skills/safety-incident-brief.md).
- **supplier-risk-watch** (Global Sourcing Manager): Scan shipment emails → flag at-risk parts → produce Excel workbook. Start from [supplier-risk-tracker](../skills/supplier-risk-tracker.md).
- **shift-start-brief** (Assembly Team Leader; Plant Operations Manager): Gather staffing, model mix, alerts → summarize → produce Word brief. Start from [shift-handover-digest](../skills/shift-handover-digest.md).
## Top 5 connectors to demo

- **ERP (confirm)** (Product Line Finance Manager; Global Sourcing Manager): Finance sees landed cost and tariff impact by SKU in one tracker.
- **Retailer portal data (confirm)** (Retail Account Manager; Demand Planner): Account manager preps a line review from POS data and emails.
- **Dynamics 365 Customer Service/Field Service** (Consumer Care Agent; Field Service Technician): Technician gets model history and parts before each home visit.
