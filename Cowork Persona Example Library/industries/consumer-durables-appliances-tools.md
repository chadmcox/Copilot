## Top 5 custom skills to build first

- **retailer-line-review-prep** (Retail Account Manager; Demand Planner): Gather POS, emails, CRM → analyze sell-through → produce Word brief and deck.
- **returns-failure-trend** (Product Quality Engineer; Product Design Engineer): Collect returns and cases → group by failure code → produce PowerPoint deck.
- **safety-incident-triage** (Product Safety & Regulatory Manager; Consumer Care Agent): Gather incident cases → assess risk signals → produce Excel tracker.
- **supplier-risk-watch** (Global Sourcing Manager): Scan shipment emails → flag at-risk parts → produce Excel tracker.
- **shift-start-brief** (Assembly Team Leader; Plant Operations Manager): Gather staffing, model mix, alerts → summarize → produce Word brief.

## Top 5 connectors to demo

- **ERP (confirm)** (Product Line Finance Manager; Global Sourcing Manager): Finance sees landed cost and tariff impact by SKU in one tracker.
- **Retailer portal data (confirm)** (Retail Account Manager; Demand Planner): Account manager preps a line review from POS data and emails.
- **Dynamics 365 Customer Service/Field Service** (Consumer Care Agent; Field Service Technician): Technician gets model history and parts before each home visit.
- **PLM (confirm)** (Product Design Engineer): Engineer summarizes ECO status for the stage-gate review.
- **QMS (confirm)** (Product Quality Engineer; Product Safety & Regulatory Manager): Quality engineer links returns to open 8Ds.
