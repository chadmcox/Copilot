## Personas and example asks

### 1. Finance

**Title:** Brand Finance Manager  
**Duty:** Own brand P&L, trade spend accruals, and forecasting.  
**Sources:** Outlook, Teams chats, meeting transcripts, SharePoint/OneDrive, calendar, Dynamics 365, Power BI; ERP (confirm; e.g., SAP S/4HANA); TPM (confirm; e.g., Salesforce CG Cloud, Exceedra)

1. *Daily:* "Pull this week's trade accrual vs. actual by customer and flag gaps." → Excel tracker
2. *Analytical:* "Analyze the gross-to-net bridge for [brand] vs. plan this quarter." → Word doc
3. *Leadership:* "Build the brand P&L review deck for the category GM." → PowerPoint deck
4. *Daily:* "Summarize open deduction disputes over $10K with owners and next steps." → Excel tracker
5. *Analytical:* "Compare COGS inflation by SKU against price-increase coverage." → Word doc
6. *Leadership:* "Draft a note to sales explaining revised trade spend guardrails." → draft email for approval

## Top 5 custom skills to build first

- **trade-spend-review** (Revenue Growth Manager, Brand Finance Manager): Gather TPM and POS data → compute promo ROI → produce deck.
- **otif-fine-tracker** (Customer Operations Specialist): Pull orders and fines → find root causes → produce Excel tracker.
- **shift-handover-brief** (Team Leader, Line Operator): Gather MES events and Teams notes → summarize → draft Teams post for approval.
- **category-review-pack** (Category Manager, Brand Manager): Gather POS and shopper data → analyze → build retailer deck.
- **supplier-risk-review** (Category Buyer): Gather supplier scorecards and emails → score risk → draft email for approval.

## Top 5 connectors to demo

- **ERP (confirm)** (Brand Finance, Category Buyer, Customer Ops): Customer ops sees at-risk orders before OTIF fines hit.
- **TPM (confirm)** (Revenue Growth Manager, Brand Finance): RGM spots underperforming promotions in minutes.
- **Syndicated POS data (confirm)** (Brand Manager, Category Manager): Category manager builds a retailer review in an hour.
- **MES (confirm)** (Team Leader, Plant Manager): Team leader gets a ready-made start-of-shift brief.
- **QMS (confirm)** (QA Manager): QA trends complaints before an audit.

## Confirm with each customer

- Which ERP, TPM, MES, QMS, PLM are in use
- Syndicated POS data licensing and access
- Frontline device access and licensing
- Retailer portal/EDI access
- Labor agreement constraints
- Regulatory scope (FDA, EPA, packaging EPR laws)
