## How to read this page

Each persona has three asks:

- **Quick win:** a small, everyday job that pays off right away.
- **Hand-off:** a multi-step job across several sources that would otherwise take an hour or more.
- **Recurring:** something scheduled or ongoing that keeps working without the person.

Every persona can use the standard Microsoft 365 sources (Outlook, Teams, meeting transcripts, SharePoint/OneDrive, calendar). **Systems** lists only the industry-specific systems that persona uses beyond those, marked *(confirm)* because they vary by company. Anything that sends, posts or schedules is a draft for the person's approval.

**Why the two industry personas:** Revenue Growth Manager: trade spend is among the largest CPG costs. Category Manager: retailer category captaincy drives shelf space and growth.

## Personas and example asks

### 1. Finance

**Title:** Brand Finance Manager  
**Duty:** Own brand P&L, trade spend accruals, and forecasting.  
**Systems:** Power BI, ERP (confirm; e.g., SAP S/4HANA), TPM (confirm; e.g., Salesforce CG Cloud, Exceedra)

- **Quick win:** "Which open deduction disputes are over $10K and who owns them?" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Build the gross-to-net bridge for [brand] vs. plan from the TPM and ERP, compare COGS inflation with price-increase coverage, and prepare the brand P&L review" → PowerPoint deck
- **Recurring:** "Every Monday, compare trade accrual vs. actual by customer and flag gaps" → scheduled digest

### 2. HR

**Title:** HR Business Partner – Supply Chain  
**Duty:** Support plant and DC workforce, talent, and engagement.  
**Systems:** HRIS (confirm; e.g., Workday)

- **Quick win:** "Which frontline reqs have been open over 30 days, by site?" → Excel workbook
- **Hand-off:** "Analyze engagement survey comments by plant and turnover against pay positioning, and prepare the talent review deck for supply chain leadership" → PowerPoint deck
- **Recurring:** "Every Friday, summarize open employee-relations cases from email and Teams" → scheduled digest

### 3. Frontline Worker

**Title:** Packaging Line Operator  
**Duty:** Run packaging lines, changeovers, and in-process quality checks.  
**Systems:** MES (confirm)

- **Quick win:** "What are the changeover steps for the new [SKU] carton?" → Word doc (one page)
- **Hand-off:** "Summarize my line's last five stops, what fixed them and the code-date verification procedure into one page" → Word doc (one page)
- **Recurring:** "At the end of each shift, draft my handover with stops, holds and open issues" → Word doc (one page)

## Top 5 custom skills to build first

- **trade-spend-review** (Revenue Growth Manager, Brand Finance Manager): Gather TPM and POS data → compute promo ROI → produce deck.
- **otif-fine-tracker** (Customer Operations Specialist): Pull orders and fines → find root causes → produce Excel workbook. Start from [customer-escalation-pack](../skills/customer-escalation-pack.md).
- **shift-handover-brief** (Team Leader, Line Operator): Gather MES events and Teams notes → summarize → draft Teams post for approval. Start from [shift-handover-digest](../skills/shift-handover-digest.md).
- **category-review-pack** (Category Manager, Brand Manager): Gather POS and shopper data → analyze → build retailer deck.
- **supplier-risk-review** (Category Buyer): Gather supplier scorecards and emails → score risk → email draft. Start from [supplier-risk-tracker](../skills/supplier-risk-tracker.md).
## Top 5 connectors to demo

- **ERP (confirm)** (Brand Finance, Category Buyer, Customer Ops): Customer ops sees at-risk orders before OTIF fines hit.
- **TPM (confirm)** (Revenue Growth Manager, Brand Finance): RGM spots underperforming promotions in minutes.
- **Syndicated POS data (confirm)** (Brand Manager, Category Manager): Category manager builds a retailer review in an hour.
- **MES (confirm)** (Team Leader, Plant Manager): Team leader gets a ready-made start-of-shift brief.
- **QMS (confirm)** (QA Manager): QA trends complaints before an audit.

## Confirm with each customer

- Which ERP, TPM, MES, QMS, PLM are in use
- Syndicated POS data licensing and access
- Frontline device access and licensing
- Retailer portal/EDI access
- Labor agreement constraints
- Regulatory scope (FDA, EPA, packaging EPR laws)
